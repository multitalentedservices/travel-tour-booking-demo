Our story · Fiction, labelled

A dastaan is a story told at dusk. We plan trips the same way.

Dastaan Journeys is a fictional operator invented for this portfolio demo — but it is written the way a credible northern-Pakistan operator actually works: departures from Islamabad, licensed local guides, capped group sizes, prices printed per person in rupees, and itineraries that say plainly what is included and what is not.

The pattern follows real operators researched for this build: one page per tour with a day-by-day itinerary, inclusions and exclusions side by side, and a booking path that never hides the price.

Why us

Four promises, printed where you can check them

Seats you can count

Every departure shows its remaining seats, and the number moves when bookings are made or cancelled — try it: book a seat, then check the tour page again.

Prices with the working shown

Adults at the printed fare, children at a stated 60%, add-ons priced flat or per traveller — the enquiry page itemises every line before you confirm.

No invented proof

Every review here is fictional and says so. A demo that fakes testimonials teaches the wrong lesson; this one labels its fiction instead.
